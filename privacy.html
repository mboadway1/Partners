Partners privacy

Partners is a private app used by two people to share photos, tasks, a calendar, money, flights, fuel stops and links. It is not offered to the public.

What it uses from Google

  • Your name, email and picture, to show who added what.
  • Google Calendar, to show and add events you choose.
  • Google Tasks (read only), to import your task lists once.
  • Google Photos, only the photos you pick in Google's photo picker.

Where it's kept

Photos and settings are kept on your own devices. Shared tasks, links, money entries and your Google sign-in are kept in the app's private relay so both partners and your TV can see them. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing your data

Sign out in Settings, and remove Partners' access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Ask the app's owner to delete anything kept in the relay.
