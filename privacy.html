Partners privacy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

Partners is an app for two people to share photos, tasks, a calendar, money, flights, fuel stops and links. Only you and your partner can see what you share in it.

What Partners keeps

  • Your account: your name, email address and, if you use one, a securely scrambled password (never the password itself).
  • What you share with your partner: tasks, links, money entries, trips, concerns and settings, kept in Partners' private storage so both of you and your TV can see them.
  • Your plan: whether you're on a trial or a paid plan. Payments are handled by Stripe, which keeps your card details; Partners never sees your full card number.

What it uses from Google (only if you connect Google)

  • Your name, email and picture, to show who added what.
  • Google Calendar, to show and add events you choose.
  • Google Tasks (read only), to import your task lists once.
  • Google Photos, only the photos you pick in Google's photo picker.

Partners' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Claude

When you ask for Claude's help (for example, wording a concern), the text you wrote is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to get a suggestion. It isn't used to show you ads.

What Partners doesn't do

Partners doesn't sell your information, doesn't show ads, and doesn't share your information except with the services above that make the app work.

Removing your data

You can log out in Settings → Account, and remove Partners' access to Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your account and everything in it, use the contact option in Settings → Account.
