Partners terms

Last updated October 12, 2026.

Partners is an app for two people to share tasks, a calendar, money notes, trips, links and photos, and to talk things through. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

Your account

You need an account to use Partners. Keep your password private. One account is for one person; a couple is two accounts joined by an invite. You're responsible for what you add to Partners.

Free trial and plans

  • New couples get 14 days free. No card is needed for the trial.
  • After the trial, Partners costs $7.99 a month or $79 a year (US dollars, plus any tax that applies). One plan covers both partners.
  • Plans renew automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel. Payments are processed by Stripe; Partners never sees or stores your full card number.
  • Prices may change; we'll tell you at least 30 days before a change affects your plan.

Canceling and refunds

You can cancel anytime in Partners under Settings → Account → Manage subscription. Partners keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. Payments already made aren't refunded, except where the law requires it.

If a plan ends

If your trial ends or your plan stops, your information is kept for 90 days so you can pick a plan and carry on. After that it may be deleted.

Claude's help

Some features use Claude, an AI model, to suggest wording or ideas. Suggestions can be wrong, so use your own judgment. There's a fair-use limit on how much Claude help you can use each day. Partners isn't therapy or medical, legal or financial advice. If you ever feel unsafe at home, contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.

Fair use

Don't use Partners to break the law, harass anyone, or try to get into accounts that aren't yours. We may suspend accounts that do.

Changes and availability

We work to keep Partners running, but it's provided "as is" without guarantees. We may update these terms; if a change matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
